5paisa

5paisa is an online trading platform that lets investors and traders invest at a lower cost through zero-brokerage and flat per-order pricing.

My role was to design the marketing website landing pages and the sign-up flow with a focus on driving demat-account registrations and improving conversion from visitor to lead.

Products

An interactive view of all the products i.e. Stocks, Derivatives, Commodities, Currencies, Mutual Funds, Digital Gold, Loans and Insurance.

Features

Be it Investors or Traders 5paisa helps it's users starting from building the portfolio to teaching them about trading and investments.

Calculators

Margin Calculator for Equity, Currency and Commodity.

APIs

5paisa helps the companies to build their own APIs which is flexible with Python, JSON, Golang.

Fund Screener

List of curated funds to help the user to select the right fund for their investment.

Algo Trading

5paisa Algo Trading helps users to trade with automated advanced trading tools.
